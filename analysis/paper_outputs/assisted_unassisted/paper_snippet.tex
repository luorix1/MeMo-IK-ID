\subsection{Estimation During Assisted and Unassisted Walking — numeric values}

Across the shared hip conditions (LG + RA), the assisted hip-exoskeleton trials produced a mean RMSE of 0.170~Nm/kg and a mean $R^2$ of 0.792, compared with 0.175~Nm/kg and 0.718 during the unassisted IMU trials (hip-only kinematics through the hip-exo TCN; $n=32$).

Across the shared knee conditions (RA + RD), the assisted knee-exoskeleton trials produced a mean RMSE of 0.192~Nm/kg and a mean $R^2$ of 0.615, compared with 0.352~Nm/kg and -0.466 for the unassisted knee-only IMU model ($n=16$).
